How SmartPark Works

Effortless parking in four simple steps from anywhere on your mobile or desktop browser.

1. Search & Filter

Locate available parking bays by zone, terminal, vehicle type, and scheduled time window.

2. Reserve Instantly

Lock in your dedicated slot with concurrency protection and transparent pricing estimates.

3. Check-In & Park

Present your digital QR booking code to the parking operator for instant gate validation.

4. Checkout & Pay

Automated duration billing with free-minute grace periods and printable digital receipts.
