Project Abstract

SmartPark – Smart Parking Management System

Tagline: Park Smarter | Save Time | Move Better

1. Executive Abstract

SmartPark is a full-stack enterprise web application designed to simplify parking management by connecting vehicle owners, parking operators, and administrators through a centralized platform. Users can register vehicles, search available parking spaces, reserve a slot, view booking details with QR passes, and track live parking status. Parking operators manage parking zones, slots, live occupancy, and active bookings, while administrators oversee users, facilities, tariff rules, and audit logs.

2. Problem Statement

In busy campuses, commercial plazas, transit hubs, and corporate complexes, drivers spend excessive time searching for vacant spaces, leading to traffic congestion and fuel waste. Traditional manual paper slips or stand-alone barrier gates make real-time occupancy tracking, reservation scheduling, and audit reconciliation cumbersome. SmartPark delivers an end-to-end digital ecosystem providing live slot visibility, concurrency-safe reservations, and automated duration billing.

Automated Sweeper

SmartPark runs an automated Spring @Scheduled background sweep every 60 seconds that detects any reserved booking that has not checked in within 15 minutes of scheduled entry time, automatically frees the parking bay, marks the booking expired, and notifies the driver.
